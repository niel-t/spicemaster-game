## Kipkruidenkwartier NPCs (the spice-war advisers)

Each one gives Eddy Jr. an idea for his Ideeënboek. Code: `browser-game/js/npcs.js`.

- **Shooki**, the cousin. Role: errand boy, extra daily job. Look: green tracksuit, chain, black cap. Personality: always "on a deal", two phones, sold ringtones once.
- **Henk Cijfer**, the bookkeeper. Role: price forecasts. Look: beige sweater, glasses, thinning hair. Personality: nervous, did Eddy Sr.'s books for 22 years, his spreadsheet was only ever wrong in 1998.
- **Slager Bram**, the butcher. Role: client and cheap Bio supplier. Look: huge, bald, moustache, white apron. Personality: cries at good lamb spice.
- **Ome Joop**, Eddy Sr.'s old grinder. Role: keeper of Dad's notebook (recipes, De Gouden Mix clues). Look: 80, flat cap, glasses, cardigan, on a bench by the canal. Personality: hearing aid whistles, feeds the ducks paprika.
- **Monteur Mo**, Dad's machine man. Role: fixes broken machines. Look: blue overall, beard, grease to the elbows. Personality: refuses to use a phone.
- **De Fluisteraar**, the rumour man. Role: sells tomorrow's market news. Look: long dark coat, hat, slick hair, moustache. Personality: whispers everything.
- **Kevin**, a fed-up Lampas. Role: warns of Lampas strikes, can be bribed. Look: orange tracksuit and cap, cigarette. Personality: paid in expired frikandellen, done with his boss.
