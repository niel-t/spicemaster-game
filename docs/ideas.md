## Customer encounters & dialogue
- **Repo mechanic:** If a customer won't pay, Eddy Jr. unbolts their shoarma grill, throws it
  in the Oofmobiel and sells it to their competitor down the street.
- **The "fake complaint" bluff:** Customers claim the last batch was "all salt, no flavour"
  to dodge their tab. Eddy Jr. can inspect their kitchen and prove they diluted it with
  cheap flour.
- **Intimidation & backup:** Some owners bring out their cousins when asked for cash. Skill
  checks (Street Smarts, Bluff, or a bribe of free garlic sauce) decide the outcome.
- **Loyal butcher perks:** High trust with traditional butchers unlocks rare ingredients or
  referrals to high-volume restaurants.

## The Oofmobiel (delivery module)
- **Van upgrades:**
  - *Heavy-duty leaf springs:* Stop the rear bumper dragging with 800 kg of spice tubs
    aboard.
  - *Tinted windows & hidden compartments:* Hide cash and stock from rival traders and the
    tax inspectors.
  - *Chip tuning:* Faster highway runs between Kaasland's cities.
- **Parking fines in city centres:** Park on the pavement with hazards on; argue too long
  inside and come back to a wheel clamp.

## Factory & recipe management
- **The "filler" dilemma:** Cut expensive spices (cardamom, nutmeg) with rusk crumbs or
  extra salt. Better short-term margins, but perceptive customers may notice.
- **Eddy Sr.'s notebook:** Family recipes unlock gradually. Cryptic notes in the shed need
  experimentation or reputation milestones before he trusts Eddy Jr. with the next one.
- **Automated packaging vs hand-scooping:** Progress from hand-scooping into plastic buckets
  in the shed to buying industrial sealing machines and hiring workers.
